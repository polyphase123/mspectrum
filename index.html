Plan Inclusions & Details

Explore the rules, inclusions, and benefits of both solar programs.

Solar Savings Plan

  • Zero Upfront Cost: Fully funded and built by MSpectrum.
  • Promo Discount: Blended rate less ₱3.00/kWh (Y1-3) PROMO
  • Succeeding Years (Y4+): Blended rate less ₱2.00/kWh.
  • Price Floor: Rate is capped at a minimum of ₱11.50/kWh.
  • Full Net-Metering Integration: Export surplus energy back for grid credits.
  • FREE Peak Off-Peak registration: Lower your regular grid bill further.
  • FREE Operations & Maintenance: Unlimited system maintenance included.

Solar Subscription Plan

  • Zero Upfront Cost: Built and fully operated by MSpectrum.
  • Fixed Subscription Fee: ₱5,999/month fixed fee for standard 5 kW system.
  • Fully Protected: Solar panel insurance is fully included in the fee.
  • Free Unlimited Operations & Maintenance: Never worry about repair costs.
  • Custom Add-ons Available: Integration for home batteries (integration labor free, battery hardware extra).
  • Grid Service Upgrade: Support for Meralco service entrance upgrades if needed.

MSpectrum Solar Home Plan Promo Mechanics

Promo Period: May 14, 2026 – June 30, 2026. Customers must sign contract on or before June 30, 2026.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Active Meralco account in good credit standing (paid at least 7 bills last year before actual disconnection).
  • Complete documentary requirements submitted.
  • Passed MSpectrum's technical and site feasibility assessment.

Savings Plan Offer Details

  • Discount Structure: Meralco blended rate less ₱3.00/kWh for first 36 months; blended rate less ₱2.00/kWh for succeeding years.
  • Price Floor: A minimum rate of ₱11.50/kWh applies at all times.
  • System Size: Minimum system size of 5 kW, subject to engineering right-sizing.
  • Ownership: MSpectrum retains system ownership, operations, and maintenance.
  • Contract Term: Perpetual contract; early termination fees apply before Year 10.

Subscription Plan Offer Details

  • Fixed Billing: Pay a fixed fee (e.g., ₱5,999/month for 5 kWp) which is offset by your Meralco billing reduction.
  • Inclusions: Insurance cover, full preventive and corrective maintenance.
  • System size: Designed for systems starting around 5 kW, ideal for consumers with ~600 kWh monthly usage.
